QUICK START GUIDE

Up and running in 60 seconds

Zero admin privileges required. Connect with your student Canvas account in 3 dead-simple steps.

01

Generate Token in Canvas

Log in to your university Canvas web portal, click Account in the sidebar → Settings → scroll down to Approved Integrations and click + New Access Token.

Name it "Modula" and copy the generated key.
02

Paste in Modula Settings

Open Modula, paste your university's Canvas URL (e.g. https://canvas.bham.ac.uk) and your access token into the Settings modal, then click Save & Connect.

Choose your download folder (default: ~/Downloads/Modula).
03

Download & Study Offline

All your enrolled courses, Panopto recordings, and assignments load instantly. Click Download Module or Download All Modules to save everything locally for exam prep.

Modula will silently auto-sync new slides in the background.

macOS Gatekeeper

Opening Modula on Mac for the first time

Because Modula is a free open-source student project built without a /yr Apple Developer certificate, macOS Gatekeeper may show "Modula is damaged and can't be opened" or "Cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software" on first launch.

EASIEST · NO TERMINAL
Method 1: System Settings "Open Anyway"
  1. Double-click Modula.app in your Applications folder.
  2. When the prompt appears, open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the Security section and click Open Anyway.
1-CLICK TERMINAL COMMAND
Method 2: Remove Quarantine Flag

Press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Enter, and paste this one-time command:

xattr -cr /Applications/Modula.app
Windows Defender

Bypassing Windows SmartScreen (2 Clicks)

When opening for the first time on Windows 10/11, Microsoft Defender SmartScreen may display: "Windows protected your PC — Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognised app from starting."

1. Click "More info"
2. Click "Run anyway"
